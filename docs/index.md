# Blakken Apprentice Guide

You look at Blakken, tell the AI what is wrong or what you want, try the result,
and correct it. When it is ready, you say "publish" or "deploy blakken api."

This guide teaches that way of working. It uses real exchanges from the cart,
toolbar, swipe actions, menu deletion, live shop updates, and releases. It does
not ask you to turn every short message into a specification.

## Read it in small stretches

Start with the first three chapters to recognize the apps and get something
running. Then follow the actual conversations. The later chapters explain what
the AI must do behind the short release requests. Finish with how to resume
the work as another maintainer.
